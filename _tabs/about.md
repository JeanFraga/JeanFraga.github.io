I'm **Jean Fraga**, a data engineer in Miami, FL. I build cloud data platforms: pipelines, lakehouses and warehouses, plus the infrastructure-as-code and CI/CD that keep them reliable. Most of my work is on Google Cloud.

## What I do now

I joined **Sanitas Medical Center** in April 2022 as a business analyst, and data engineering was part of the job from the start (Python scripts, ETL requirements for dashboards). Since early 2026 I've been working full-time as a data engineer under the Chief Data and Applications Officer. Highlights:

- **Pipelines that replaced spreadsheets.** I replaced manual Excel reporting with automated Python and BigQuery pipelines. Key-based incremental loads upsert 100K+ rows per run into multi-million-row tables, and refreshes went from monthly to weekly.
- **Value-based care analytics.** I built clinical-outcome pipelines that reconcile EMR data against payer claims, plus XGBoost and NLP forecasts of visit volume and staffing. These informed spending, staffing, insurance and value-based-care bonus decisions.
- **Engineering practices.** I was the first at Sanitas to adopt source control, dbt and Terraform. I moved code off shared drives and manual deploys onto Azure Synapse with Azure DevOps, and led the company's first Google Cloud team.
- **Data quality.** I enforced metadata validation and threshold-based run failures across 10 sources, so errors get caught before they reach users.
- **Dashboards and automation.** I delivered ~30 Looker, QlikSense and QuickSight dashboards to ~50 clinical, operations, finance and executive users. My Python automations save 10+ hours a month.
